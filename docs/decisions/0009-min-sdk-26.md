# 0009. Minimum SDK 26

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-07, recommended in the bootstrap session and confirmed by the owner

## Context

The minimum SDK trades reach against the cost of supporting old platform behaviour. The app is
animation-heavy and handles dates constantly.

Public distribution figures from late 2025 put Android 9 (API 28) and newer at about 95 percent
of active devices, with Android 8.0 and 8.1 adding roughly one to two percent on top. Minimum
SDK 26 therefore reaches roughly 96 to 97 percent of devices. These are third-party summaries
of Google's dashboard and should be read as approximate.

## Decision

The minimum SDK is 26 (Android 8.0).

## Consequences

- `java.time` is available natively, with no core library desugaring.
- Adaptive launcher icons and notification channels are always present, so there are no
  pre-26 fallbacks for them.
- Resource folders qualified with `-v26` or lower are redundant, and Lint rejects them.
- Roughly three to four percent of devices cannot install the app.
- APIs newer than 26 still need version checks or AndroidX equivalents; Lint enforces this.
- Raising the minimum later is easy. Lowering it is not, so it should not be lowered.
