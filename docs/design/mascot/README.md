# Troško, approved sketches

The four expressions of the mascot as the owner approved them on 2026-10-07, in the light
theme's colours. They are sketches that fix the character, not finished artwork: the app does
not use these files.

| File | Expression | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `trosko-neutral.svg` | looking at you sideways | the default |
| `trosko-delighted.svg` | arms up | after a save, now and then |
| `trosko-dozing.svg` | asleep | an empty period |
| `trosko-startled.svg` | hands on his head | something went wrong |

What makes him Troško, and must survive into the final artwork: the lopsided outline, one eye
bigger than the other, the raised eyebrow, the thin limbs, and a rim that is the same hard
shadow every button has. Known weakness of the sketches: the arms are thin and partly hidden
behind the body.

The character is described in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md#troško-the-mascot).
